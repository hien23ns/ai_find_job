Our Company

We're Hitachi Digital Services, a global digital solutions and transformation business with a bold vision of our world's potential. We're people-centric and here to power good. Every day, we future-proof urban spaces, conserve natural resources, protect rainforests, and save lives. This is a world where innovation, technology, and deep expertise come together to take our company and customers from what's now to what's next. We make it happen through the power of acceleration.

Imagine the sheer breadth of talent it takes to bring a better tomorrow closer to today. We don't expect you to 'fit' every requirement – your life experience, character, perspective, and passion for achieving great things in the world are equally as important to us.

Requirements

Job Description

Pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Technology, or a related field.
Good communication skills in English (Japanese is a plus)
GPA of 3.0/4.0 or above.
Good programming knowledge in at least one of the following technology stacks: Python, Java, Node.js/TypeScript, or C#/.NET.
Basic understanding of data structures, databases, REST APIs, operating systems, and client-server architecture.
Familiarity with Git or another version control system.
Experience completing at least one academic, personal, or open-source software project.
Basic understanding of at least one Cloud platform such as AWS, Azure, or GCP.
Interest in Cloud Engineering, DevOps, automation, system reliability, and technical troubleshooting.
Familiarity with AI-assisted development tools such as GitHub Copilot, Claude, Cursor, or similar.
Ability to explain, validate, test, and troubleshoot code or configurations created with AI assistance.
Strong analytical, problem-solving, teamwork, ownership, and continuous learning mindset.

Preferred Qualifications

Exposure to Linux command-line environments or scripting with Python, Bash, or PowerShell.
Familiarity with Docker, Docker Compose, or container concepts.
Exposure to CI/CD, networking, application deployment, or infrastructure automation.
Personal or academic projects involving backend development, REST APIs, databases, system integration, deployment, or automation.
Ability to work effectively in a collaborative and fast-paced environment.
